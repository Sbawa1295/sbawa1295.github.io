Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Introduction

BC Driving Knowledge Test Prep ("we", "our", or "the app") is an independent study app built by Surrey Web Guru Design Ltd. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects — which, in short, is almost nothing.

The App Works Offline

BC Driving Knowledge Test Prep does not require an account, a login, or an internet connection to use. Your quiz answers, progress, starred questions, missed-question history, and test-date reminders are all stored locally on your device using Apple's standard on-device storage. None of this information is sent to us, to any server we operate, or to any third party. We have no way to see your progress, your answers, or how you use the app.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • We do not use analytics, advertising IDs, or any third-party tracking software
  • We do not show ads or use any ad network
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not sell or share any user data

Notifications

If you set a knowledge test date in the app, you can choose to allow local reminder notifications (for example, "2 weeks until your test"). These reminders are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device through Apple's notification system. No test-date information is sent anywhere — reminders work even with your phone in airplane mode.

In-App Purchases

The app offers optional paid access (weekly, monthly, or one-time lifetime) through Apple's App Store in-app purchase system (StoreKit). Purchases are processed entirely by Apple; we do not receive or store your payment details. Apple's own privacy policy governs how purchase and payment information is handled.

Audio / Listening Feature

The app can read questions and lessons aloud using your device's built-in text-to-speech, or pre-recorded audio bundled inside the app. This audio plays entirely on your device and is never uploaded or transmitted anywhere.

Data Storage

All progress, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device. No data is uploaded to external servers.

Children's Privacy

This app is a general-audience study tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because the app does not collect personal information from any user.

Security

Since we do not collect or transmit personal data, the risk to your privacy is minimal. All app operations are performed locally on your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

About This App

BC Driving Knowledge Test Prep is an independent study app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the ICBC (the Insurance Corporation of British Columbia) or the Government of British Columbia.
