Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 5, 2026

Introduction

Router Login Pro ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Network Information: The app accesses your device's network information (WiFi SSID, gateway IP address, local IP address) solely to help you connect to your router's admin panel. This information is processed locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

Location Permission: We request location permission solely because Android requires it to read WiFi network names (SSID). We do not track, store, or transmit your location data.

Advertising: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google may collect certain device information and advertising identifiers to serve relevant ads. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more details: https://policies.google.com/privacy

In-App Purchases: Purchase transactions are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not collect or store any payment information.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • We do not collect router passwords or login credentials
  • We do not collect browsing history or router activity
  • We do not collect or transmit WiFi passwords
  • We do not track your physical location
  • We do not sell any user data to third parties

Data Storage

All network scanning results, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device. No data is uploaded to external servers. The router credentials database (default usernames and passwords) is publicly available manufacturer information bundled within the app.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements (free version only)
  • Google Play Billing — for processing in-app purchases
  • ip-api.com — for IP geolocation lookups (when you use the IP Tools feature)
  • api.ipify.org — for detecting your public IP address

Network Scanning

The Network Scanner and Port Scanner tools operate entirely on your local network. Scan results are displayed on-screen and are not stored permanently or transmitted anywhere.

Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

We are committed to protecting your information. Since we do not collect or transmit personal data, the risk to your privacy is minimal. All network operations are performed locally on your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
